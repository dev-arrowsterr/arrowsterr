# Tracking script: notice for customers

Customers who install the Arrowsterr tracking script can paste this into their own privacy policy.

---

**Website analytics**

We use Arrowsterr to understand how people find and use this website, including visits that come from AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Arrowsterr records pages viewed, the site you came from, your device and browser type, your country, how far you scroll, and clicks on buttons and links. It does not use cookies, does not record what you type into forms, and does not store your full IP address. The data is used only for statistics about this website. Arrowsterr processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding and improving our website.

---

## Do customers need a cookie banner for it?

In most cases, no. The script sets no cookies and stores nothing on the visitor's device, so the EU cookie consent rule (ePrivacy) usually does not apply to it. Customers still need to mention it in their privacy policy, as above. Some countries' regulators read the rules more strictly. A customer who already asks for consent for all analytics can load the script only after consent.
