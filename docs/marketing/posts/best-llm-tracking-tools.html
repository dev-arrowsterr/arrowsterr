What is LLM tracking?

LLM tracking means checking, on a schedule, what AI models say when people ask about your category. You pick the questions your buyers ask, and the tool asks them for you, reads every answer and records each brand it names.

Over time, that gives you a clear picture of:

  • Mentions: how often each AI names your brand.
  • Position: where you land in the list. First is worth a lot more than fifth.
  • Sentiment: whether the AI describes you warmly, neutrally or with doubts.
  • Competitors: which brands show up when you don’t.
  • Sources: the websites the AI pulled from to build its answer.

It works like Google rank tracking with one big difference. An AI gives a single answer, so you’re either in it or left out.

How LLM tracking tools work

  1. You add prompts. These are the questions your buyers ask, like “best project management tool for agencies.” Better tools suggest prompts for you.
  2. The tool asks each model. Some tools use each AI company’s API, while others use a browser that acts like a real person. Both methods work. What matters most is how often the checks run.
  3. It reads the answers. The tool finds every brand in each answer, in order, and matches spelling variations so “Notion AI” and “Notion” count as one brand.
  4. It scores and charts the results. You get mention rate, average position and sentiment, broken down by topic and by model, with a trend line over time.

Treat any single day as noise. Thirty days of daily checks gives you a signal you can act on.

What to look for in an LLM tracking tool

  1. Model coverage. ChatGPT is a must, and if it’s the only model you care about, my guide to the best ChatGPT rank trackers goes deeper. Google AI Overviews matter if you get Google traffic, and Gemini, Claude and Perplexity are worth having too. Check which models cost extra.
  2. Check frequency. Daily is best. Weekly is the minimum.
  3. Prompt suggestions. A tool that researches your market and suggests prompts saves you hours of setup.
  4. Full answers. You should be able to read the exact answer each model gave.
  5. Sources. Seeing which sites the AI cites tells you where to get mentioned next.
  6. A way to act. The most useful tools help you plan and write the content that closes your gaps.

How to pick prompts worth tracking

A tracker is only as useful as the prompts you give it. Here’s how I build a starting list.

  1. Start with buying questions. “Best [category] for [type of company].” “[Competitor] alternatives.” “Is [your brand] worth it?” These are the prompts where a mention turns into a sale.
  2. Mine your own data. Export your queries from Google Search Console and look for long, question-style searches. Your sales call notes and support tickets are full of these questions too.
  3. Group prompts into topics. Small wording changes can flip an AI’s answer. Group 5 to 10 related prompts into a topic and track the topic as a whole.
  4. Start small. Twenty-five to fifty prompts across five topics is plenty to begin with. Add more once you know which topics bring in buyers.

What to do once you have the data

Tracking only pays off when you act on it. Here’s the loop I run. For software built around the fixing side, see my roundup of the best GEO tools.

  1. Find the losing topics. Look for topics where competitors get named and you don’t.
  2. Read the sources. Check which sites the AI cites for those prompts. Those are the pages you need to appear on, from review sites and Reddit threads to “best tools” lists.
  3. Write the missing pages. Comparison pages, alternatives pages and “best X for Y” pages get cited often. Fill them with plain facts the AI can quote.
  4. Wait, then measure. Give each change two to four weeks, then check the trend for that topic.

FAQ

Is LLM tracking the same as AI visibility tracking?

Mostly, yes. LLM tracking, AI visibility, AI rank tracking and GEO tracking all describe the same job: seeing how AI models talk about your brand.

Which LLMs should I track?

Start with ChatGPT, since it has the most users, and add Google AI Overviews if you get Google traffic. After that, add Gemini, Perplexity and Claude.

How often should a tool check?

Daily is ideal, and weekly is the minimum. Answers move a lot, so you need enough checks to see the trend.

Can I track LLM mentions for free?

You can check by hand, though it won’t scale. Google Analytics 4 can show visits from sites like chatgpt.com and perplexity.ai, and AthenaHQ has a free tier with a small number of credits. Most paid tools offer a free trial.

What is the cheapest LLM tracking tool?

Keyword.com’s credit-based AI Visibility plan starts at $9.80/month ($7.83 billed yearly). Arrowsterr (50 prompts, 6 AIs) and Otterly.ai (15 prompts, 4 AIs) both start at $29/month.

How many prompts should I track?

Start with 25 to 50 buying questions across five topics, with 5 to 10 related prompts per topic.
