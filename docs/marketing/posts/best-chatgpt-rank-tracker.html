What does it mean to rank in ChatGPT?

ChatGPT doesn’t show ten blue links. When someone asks “what’s the best email tool for a small shop?”, it writes a single answer, often with a short list of brands.

Your “rank” is where you appear in that list. Being named first is the top spot. Being named fifth still counts, though fewer people act on it. Not being named at all is like sitting on page two of Google.

A ChatGPT rank tracker measures three things over time:

  • Mention rate: the share of answers that name you. If 6 of 10 answers include you, your mention rate is 60%.
  • Average position: where you land in the list when you’re named.
  • Sentiment: whether ChatGPT describes you in a good light.

How ChatGPT decides which brands to name

Nobody outside OpenAI knows the full recipe, but after watching thousands of answers, a few patterns are clear.

What it learned in training

ChatGPT learned from a huge amount of text on the web. Brands that were written about often, in many places, are more likely to come to mind. That’s why big, well-known names show up even when they aren’t the best fit.

What it finds when it searches

For many questions, especially about products and prices, ChatGPT searches the web before it answers. It reads a handful of pages and builds its reply from them. If those pages mention you, you have a good shot at the list. If they don’t, you probably won’t make it.

How the question is worded

“Best CRM for startups” and “which CRM should a five-person startup use” can return two different lists. So for each topic, track a small group of similar prompts. A single prompt gives you a noisy reading.

How to check your ChatGPT rank by hand

Before you pay for anything, run this quick check yourself. It takes about ten minutes.

  1. Open a temporary chat. In ChatGPT, start a temporary chat so it ignores your memory and past chats. That gets you closer to what a new buyer sees.
  2. Ask five buying questions. Use the words your buyers use. For example: “What’s the best [category] for [type of company]?” and “What are the best alternatives to [competitor]?”
  3. Write down every brand, in order. Note where you show up, or whether you show up at all.
  4. Look at the sources. If ChatGPT searched the web, it will show links. Note which sites it used.
  5. Repeat in a few days. The list will change. That’s normal, and it’s why one check isn’t enough.

When to stop checking by hand

Once you want more than a handful of prompts, daily checks, competitor data or a trend line, a tracker pays for itself in saved time.

How to pick the best ChatGPT rank tracker

  1. Daily checks. ChatGPT’s answers move a lot, so you need daily data to see a real trend.
  2. Full answer history. A score tells you where you stand. Reading what ChatGPT actually said tells you why.
  3. Sources. The pages ChatGPT cites are your to-do list.
  4. More than ChatGPT. Your buyers also see Google’s AI Overviews and use Gemini and Perplexity. My guide to LLM tracking tools covers trackers built for that.
  5. Help with next steps. The best tools also help you plan and write the content that gets you named.

How to move up in ChatGPT’s answers

Once your tracker shows where you’re missing, here’s what I’d do, in this order.

  1. Get on the pages ChatGPT reads. Open your tracker’s source report and list the sites ChatGPT cites for your topics. Ask happy customers for reviews, join Reddit threads honestly and pitch the writers of “best tools” lists.
  2. Publish the pages buyers ask for. “Best [category] for [audience]”, “[Competitor] alternatives”, “[Your brand] vs [competitor]” and a clear pricing page.
  3. Write facts ChatGPT can quote. Say who you’re for, what you cost and what you do better, with numbers. Vague copy gives ChatGPT nothing to work with.
  4. Improve your search rankings. When ChatGPT searches the web, pages that already rank well are easier for it to find. Refresh pages sitting in positions 4 to 20.
  5. Measure every change. Give each change two to four weeks, then check the trend for that topic.

FAQ

Can I see my ChatGPT rankings in Google Search Console?

No. Search Console only covers Google. ChatGPT doesn’t share which questions showed your brand, so you need a tracker that asks the questions for you.

Can I see traffic from ChatGPT?

Yes. In Google Analytics 4, go to Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic acquisition. Switch to “Session source / medium” and look for chatgpt.com. That shows visits, though it won’t tell you how often you were named.

Why does ChatGPT give me different answers than my tracker?

ChatGPT changes its answers from one ask to the next, and your own account can shape the answer through memory and past chats. Trackers ask from a clean slate, many times over, so their numbers are more reliable than a single check.

How long does it take to rank higher in ChatGPT?

It depends on what you change. New mentions on sites ChatGPT already cites can show up within weeks. Building a brand ChatGPT knows from training takes longer.

What is the cheapest ChatGPT rank tracker?

Keyword.com, at $9.80 a month for its credit-based AI Visibility plan ($7.83 billed yearly). Arrowsterr and Otterly.ai come next at $29 a month.

Which ChatGPT rank trackers offer a free trial?
