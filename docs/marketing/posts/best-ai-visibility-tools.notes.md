# best-ai-visibility-tools: rewrite notes

**SEO title (54 chars):** 10 Best AI Visibility Tools: Does ChatGPT Recommend You?

**Meta description / excerpt (146 chars):** The best AI visibility tools compared: what 10 trackers cover, what they really cost and where each falls short. Prices checked October 2026.

## What changed and why

- **Intro rebuilt.** It now opens with the buyer behaviour and the "does AI recommend us?" question. "Best AI visibility tools" appears in sentence three, inside the first 50 words. The audience (marketing teams and agencies choosing a tool) and the October 2026 price check are stated up front, and the "giddy" opener is gone.
- **Added a "Trade-off" line to every paid tool.** Each one comes only from facts already in the post: weekly Claude/Perplexity checks on Arrowsterr, SE Ranking's lack of Claude, Profound's ChatGPT-only Starter, credits on Keyword.com and AthenaHQ, and so on. "Best for" stays in each aw-facts box. This also makes the Arrowsterr disclosure more believable.
- **Removed repeated text.** Repeated caveats moved out of the pricing lines (Keyword.com credits, Rank Prompt prompt counts) and into the trade-offs. Prose that only repeated the "Best for" box was cut.
- **Retitled some H2s for search fit.** "AI visibility tools compared at a glance", "What will it cost? Compare plans by prompt count", "AI visibility tools FAQ" and "Final thoughts on choosing an AI visibility tool". The tool H2 order is unchanged. "The 10 best AI visibility tools in 2026" is kept as the main keyword H2.
- **Added 3 internal links, one to each sister post.** LLM tracking tools goes in the definition, ChatGPT rank trackers in the Profound trade-off, and GEO tools in the "get recommended" intro.
- **Added 2 FAQs, both answered only from facts in the post.** One covers the cheapest tool, the other the tool that tracks the most models. Both target long-tail and People Also Ask queries. The answer that said "30-day trends, not single days" was rewritten to drop that phrasing.
- **Stronger CTA.** The closing paragraph points readers to a tool by need (SEO suite or tracking-to-content). The Snapshot CTA leads with a bold hook: "see where you stand before you pay for anything."
- **Fixed the table/title mismatch.** The comparison table lists 9 tools because GA4 is #10. The intro line under the filters now says so, so readers don't think a tool is missing.

**Unchanged:** every aw-* widget, data-* attribute, table, img, figure, aw-facts block, the calculator JSON and all prices. Visible word count went from about 3,450 to 3,800. Most of the increase is the nine trade-off lines and the two new FAQs.
