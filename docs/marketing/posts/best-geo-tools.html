What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization is the work of getting your brand mentioned and cited in AI-written answers. “Generative engines” are tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode. They answer the question in their own words.

GEO overlaps a lot with SEO, since pages that rank well often get cited by AI too. It also adds a few new jobs:

  • tracking what AI says about you, since no AI company shares that data,
  • finding the outside sites AI trusts, like review sites, forums and lists,
  • writing pages with clear facts AI can quote,
  • making sure AI crawlers can reach and read your site.

How to choose a GEO tool

  1. The right models. ChatGPT plus Google’s AI Overviews covers most buyers, and Gemini, Perplexity and Claude are good extras. Check which ones cost more. If ChatGPT is the one you care about, see my picks for the best ChatGPT rank tracker.
  2. Frequent checks. AI answers shift from day to day, so daily checks give you a trend you can trust.
  3. Source reports. The pages AI cites tell you exactly where to get mentioned.
  4. Prompt help. A tool that suggests the questions buyers ask saves you a lot of guesswork.
  5. A path to action. Charts alone won’t change what AI says, so look for content plans, briefs or writing help.

6. Google Search Console (free)

Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode draw on Google’s index, so your pages need to be indexed first, and Search Console shows you whether they are. It’s also my favorite source of prompts. In the Performance report, filter queries by words like “best,” “vs,” “how” and “which.” You’ll find the long questions people now ask AI as well. What it can’t show is the AI answer itself.

7. Bing Webmaster Tools (free)

Microsoft Copilot is built on Bing, and other tools use Bing’s results too. Bing Webmaster Tools lets you submit your sitemap, check indexing and see your Bing search data. Setup is quick, because you can import your sites straight from Google Search Console.

8. Screaming Frog SEO Spider (free up to 500 URLs)

AI crawlers have to reach your pages before AI can cite them. Screaming Frog crawls your site the way a bot does and flags blocked pages, broken links, thin pages and missing titles. The free version stops at 500 URLs, so larger sites will outgrow it. While you’re at it, check your robots.txt by hand to make sure you aren’t blocking AI crawlers like GPTBot or OAI-SearchBot by mistake.

9. Google Analytics 4 (free)

GA4 can’t tell you how often AI names you, but it does show who clicks through. Go to Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic acquisition. Switch the dimension to “Session source / medium” and look for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com.

How to write pages that AI cites

  • Answer first. Put the direct answer in the first sentence under each heading.
  • Use real facts. Prices, numbers, names and dates are easy to quote. “Industry-leading” isn’t.
  • Use question headings. Headings like “How much does X cost?” match the way people ask AI.
  • Keep it current. Update key pages often and show the date.
  • Add tables. AI can read and pull from comparison tables with ease.

GEO tools FAQ

What is the best GEO tool?

It depends on your team. Arrowsterr covers tracking and content from $29/month, Peec AI suits Looker Studio reporting, Profound fits enterprise brands, AthenaHQ has a free tier and SE Ranking adds AI tracking to a full SEO suite.

How much do GEO tools cost?

Starting prices for the paid trackers here range from $29 a month (Arrowsterr) to $295 (AthenaHQ Starter). AthenaHQ also has a free Essential tier with 300 credits, and Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and GA4 are free.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. AI tools that search the web often find pages that already rank well, so strong SEO helps your GEO.

What’s the difference between GEO and AEO?

Very little. AEO stands for answer engine optimization, and most teams use the two terms for the same work.

Can I do GEO for free?

Yes, to start. Use Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, GA4, the free version of Screaming Frog and AthenaHQ’s free tier. Most paid trackers also offer a free trial.

How long does GEO take to work?

New mentions on sites AI already cites can show up in answers within a few weeks. Bigger shifts take months of steady work.

Which GEO tool should you pick?

GEO isn’t a mystery. The best GEO tools support a habit good SEO teams already have: measure, find the gaps, fix them and measure again. The only new part is that AI answers are now part of what you measure. Pick the tracker that fits your team, then pair it with the four free tools above.
